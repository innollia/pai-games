# How to play

1. **Watch the blade.** The swordsman raises his blade toward one side. Faint ghost blades show the path it will take.
2. **BLOCK.** Hold [BLOCK] and swipe toward the blade (← → ↑ ↓). A block opens a green box: the opening.
3. **STRIKE.** Hold [STRIKE] and swipe toward the green box before it closes. Striking when there is no opening is a WHIFF: you lose 1 HP and freeze for a moment.
4. **PARRY.** The blade turns **red** when it is close enough. Block while it is red to PARRY: time slows, the opening lasts longer and your next strike deals **x2** damage. Blocking an empty opening after a parry wastes it.
5. **Read each swordsman.**
   - Thrust: straight lunges.
   - Feint: a trembling blade switches sides halfway. Watch it to the end.
   - Twin-Cut: two cuts to the same side. Parry **both** to open a real gap.
   - Iai: eyes flash, then an instant draw. Striking first is a whiff. Once drawn he keeps slashing until someone lands a hit.
   - Yellow blade behind a white one = a CHAIN attack. Wait for it.
6. **Pick a skill** after every win. HP does not refill on its own, so choose between healing and getting stronger.
7. The 9th foe, the Sword Demon, cannot be beaten. Survive as long as you can and pile up damage.
