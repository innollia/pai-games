# Title
Blade Path

# Short description
One road, nine swordsmen. Read the blade, parry at the last instant, and strike before the opening closes.

# Long description
A one-on-one sword duel game about speed of reading and reaction. Each swordsman telegraphs where the blade will come from: watch it, swipe toward it and BLOCK. Wait until the last instant — when the blade turns red — and your block becomes a PARRY: time slows, the opening stays longer, and your counter-strike deals double damage.

Every foe fights differently. The Thrust Swordsman lunges straight in. The Feint Swordsman's blade trembles when it is about to switch sides. The Twin-Cut Swordsman strikes the same side twice, and only parrying both cuts opens a real gap. The Iai Swordsman stands still, flashes his eyes, draws in an instant, then keeps slashing until one of you lands a hit and he sheathes again. Directions are shuffled every combo, so you cannot memorize — you have to read.

Every run starts bare-handed. HP never refills on its own: after each win, pick one of three skills for this run only (more HP, a heavier strike, a wider parry window, healing...). Survive eight swordsmen and face the Sword Demon, who cannot be beaten — only endured. How much damage can you pile up?

Every swordsman you defeat drops coins (nyang). Spend them in the Upgrade tab of the lobby on a permanent skill tree — Body, Blade and Eye, three tiers each. Every upgrade costs much more than the last, so choose carefully.

# Controls
Two buttons: BLOCK and STRIKE. Hold a button and swipe toward the blade (left/right/up/down) to aim and act. Block when the blade arrives, parry when it turns red, then STRIKE the green opening. Best on a phone in portrait with two thumbs. Keyboard: arrow keys / WASD to aim, J = Block, K = Strike. Sound on!
Language button (top right of the lobby): English / 한국어. Settings: sound and vibration (Android only).

# How to play

1. **Watch the blade.** The swordsman raises his blade toward one side. Faint ghost blades show the path it will take.
2. **BLOCK.** Hold [BLOCK] and swipe toward the blade (← → ↑ ↓). A block opens a green box: the opening.
3. **STRIKE.** Hold [STRIKE] and swipe toward the green box before it closes. Striking when there is no opening is a WHIFF: you lose 1 HP and freeze for a moment.
4. **PARRY.** The blade turns **red** when it is close enough. Block while it is red to PARRY: time slows, the opening lasts longer and your next strike deals **x2** damage. Blocking an empty opening after a parry wastes it.
5. **Read each swordsman.**
   - Thrust: straight lunges.
   - Feint: a trembling blade switches sides halfway. Watch it to the end.
   - Twin-Cut: two cuts to the same side. Parry **both** to open a real gap.
   - Iai: eyes flash, then an instant draw. Striking first is a whiff. Once drawn he keeps slashing until someone lands a hit.
   - Yellow blade behind a white one = a CHAIN attack. Wait for it.
6. **Pick a skill** after every win. HP does not refill on its own, so choose between healing and getting stronger.
7. The 9th foe, the Sword Demon, cannot be beaten. Survive as long as you can and pile up damage.
8. **Upgrade.** Defeated swordsmen give coins. Spend them in the lobby's Upgrade tab on permanent upgrades. Each one costs more than the last.
