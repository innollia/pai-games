# Title
Blade Path

# Short description
One road, nine swordsmen. Read the blade, parry at the last instant, and strike before the opening closes.

# Long description
A one-on-one sword duel game about speed of reading and reaction. Each swordsman telegraphs where the blade will come from: watch it, swipe toward it and BLOCK. Wait until the last instant — when the blade turns red — and your block becomes a PARRY: time slows, the opening stays longer, and your counter-strike deals double damage.

Every foe fights differently. The Thrust Swordsman lunges straight in. The Feint Swordsman's blade trembles when it is about to switch sides. The Twin-Cut Swordsman strikes the same side twice, and only parrying both cuts opens a real gap. The Iai Swordsman stands still, flashes his eyes, draws in an instant, then keeps slashing until one of you lands a hit and he sheathes again. Directions are shuffled every combo, so you cannot memorize — you have to read.

Every run starts bare-handed. HP never refills on its own: after each win, pick one of three skills for this run only (more HP, a heavier strike, a wider parry window, healing...). Survive eight swordsmen and face the Sword Demon, who cannot be beaten — only endured. How much damage can you pile up?

# Controls
Two buttons: BLOCK and STRIKE. Hold a button and swipe toward the blade (left/right/up/down) to aim and act. Block when the blade arrives, parry when it turns red, then STRIKE the green opening. Best on a phone in portrait with two thumbs. Keyboard: arrow keys / WASD to aim, J = Block, K = Strike. Sound on!
Settings (top right of the lobby): sound, vibration (Android only), and language (English / 한국어).
