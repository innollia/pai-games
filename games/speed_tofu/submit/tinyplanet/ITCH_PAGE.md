# Title
Tiny Planets / 작은 행성

# Short description
Run fast enough to leave the ground, then fly between tiny planets on real gravity to help their people.

# Long description
Five tiny planets, two moons and a burning star hang in one bright sky. There is no warp and no auto-travel: the only way to another world is your own speed. Run along a planet that is only a few steps around, and the faster you go the lighter you get, until your feet leave the ground. Jump at the right moment and you break the planet's pull and sail out into the sky.

Every planet pulls only within its own reach. Enter a neighbour's pull and your path bends: dip into its air and you spiral around it and touch down; miss the air and you whip past it in a gravity slingshot. Miss everything and you drift off into empty space. When you run fast, a dotted line in front of your feet shows where a jump would take you right now: green reaches another world, red means danger. The bar at the top turns green when a jump will escape. Once you earn the sail, you get one mid-flight turn to fix a bad launch.

Five people need your help, one on each planet: run into the wind for the windmill keeper and win a sail, drop a parcel onto a walled house on the chimney moon, carry a glass flower to a friend without landing hard enough to crack it, match the speed of the sunset band on an icy world for a glass dome, and finally reach the rose planet. The direct route there is blocked by a red rock belt; skim the burning star and let its gravity fling you around it. Red is always danger: the burning star, the rock belt and falling meteors. You have three lives, refilled whenever you land on a new planet.

# Controls
Left stick: run (push further to run faster). Jump button: jump; in the air, jump once more to turn with the sail (after you earn it) toward the stick direction. Drop button: release what you carry onto the yellow spot below. Portrait 720×1280, touch controls. Sound on!
