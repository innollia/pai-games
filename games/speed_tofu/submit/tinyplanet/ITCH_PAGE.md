# Title
The Little Courier / 어린 배달부

# Short description
Your run speed and jump moment become your flight orbit. Fly between tiny planets on real gravity and deliver fragile packages.

# Long description
Your run is your rocket. Run along a planet only a few steps around, and the speed you build and the moment you jump become your flight path through the sky. No warp, no auto-travel: aim with your feet.

Land by orbit. Every planet pulls only within its own reach. Dip into a neighbour's air and you curve around it and touch down; miss the air and its gravity slings you onward. Skim the burning star to bend around a spiked mine fence.

Fragile cargo. Glass flowers and glass domes crack if you land hard and shatter if you slam down, so circle in gently.

You are Posty, a little living mailbox with big eyes and noodle legs. Five people on five planets need help: wake the windmill by sprinting on the ground and earn the Old Glidewing, drop a parcel into a walled house on the chimney moon, carry a glass flower to a friend, match the speed of the sunset band for Big Bro, and bring a glass dome to the rose.

While you run, a dotted path shows where a jump would take you: green reaches another world, red means danger. In the air, a pale path shows where the Old Glidewing would take you. Tap GLIDE to steer toward your joystick (no stick = toward your goal). Your current task is always at the top, with an arrow when the goal is off screen. Fly off into nothing and you are towed back to your last planet ("Lost in space") — no life lost. Red is always danger. Three lives, refilled on each new planet.

English by default; Korean with the language button on the title screen.

# Controls
Left stick: run (push further = faster). Jump button: jump. In the air: GLIDE button (or Jump) once to steer. Five requests, any order; tap the list button (top left) to see them. PC: WASD/arrows, Space, F, Tab. Drop button: drop what you carry onto the yellow spot below. Portrait 720×1280, touch. Sound on!
