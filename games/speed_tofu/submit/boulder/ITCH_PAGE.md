# Title
Roll, Temple Stone! / 굴러라 신전석

# Short description
Grow, split and launch a runaway temple stone through eleven bright regions to the sea.

# Long description
Escape an ancient temple and roll to a seaside shrine. Crush pots to grow, but carry too much stone and climbs, turns and jumps become harder. Speed is your lifeline: spend 1.5 seconds below the red mark and your run ends.

Leap aqueduct gaps, discover the lower route, shed weight before fragile bridges, grow on snow, and throw pieces of yourself through purple crystal walls. The new Tide Garden pushes your stone sideways: resist with hold or jump free. At the new Resonance Altar, hold on gold pressure beds until they turn green, then release to launch over wide gaps. Each new region ends with a mixing section combining its mechanic with earlier moves.

The final sea shrine combines snow, throwing, bridges, currents and charged launches. Arrive at the sea for an ending with time, score and medal. Beat 3:00 for gold or 4:00 for silver; every finish earns bronze. Local best time and score give you another target. Retry the current zone while retaining elapsed play time, or start a fresh timed journey.

밝은 구아슈 신전에서 바다까지. 물살을 버티고, 황금 발판을 눌렀다 놓아 날아가자!

# Controls
Drag to steer; tap action buttons. Hold brakes on ground and dives in air. On a gold pressure bed, hold until at least 40% charge, then release. Portrait 720×1280, touch controls. Sound on!
