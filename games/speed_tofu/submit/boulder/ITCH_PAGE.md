# Title
Rock and Stone

# Short description
Everything you crush makes you bigger, and bigger means slower. Break off pieces of yourself to stay fast, and race a runaway temple stone to the sea.

# Long description
A speed game about weight. Crushing pots makes your temple stone bigger, but every bit of mass costs speed on climbs, turns and jumps, so you split off chunks, throw them, or slam down to stay fast. Hidden shortcuts open only to players who arrive fast enough or use the right move. Speed is your lifeline: spend 1.5 seconds below the red mark and your run ends.

Leap aqueduct gaps. Miss one and you keep falling, down past the canyon walls, until you land in a sleeping temple city far below. Grab its gems and ride the water column back up. SPLIT sheds weight and gives a short dash through houses, statues and ice walls. On the cliffs, blue-capped towers and golden idols break only when you HOLD to slam down from the air, and each one speeds you up. Grow on snow, throw pieces of yourself through purple crystal walls, and check the MAX sign before each rope bridge: too heavy and the planks snap under you. The Tide Garden pushes you sideways: HOLD to resist or JUMP free. At the Echo Altar, HOLD on gold pads until they turn green, then release to launch over wide gaps. Skill regions end with a mix section that combines the new move with earlier ones.

The final Sea Temple combines snow, throwing, bridges, currents and charged launches. Arrive at the sea for an ending with time, score and medal. Beat 3:00 for gold or 4:00 for silver; every finish earns bronze. Local best time and score give you another target. Retry the current zone while retaining elapsed play time, or start a fresh timed journey.

# Controls
Drag to steer. Action buttons (JUMP, SPLIT, HOLD, THROW) appear on the right as you unlock them. HOLD brakes on the ground, slams in the air, and charges gold pads (release when green). Best on a phone in portrait with two thumbs; with a mouse you can steer or press a button, but not both at once. Sound on!
