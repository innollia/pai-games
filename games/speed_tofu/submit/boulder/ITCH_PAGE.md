Shrinestone

# Long description
A temple stone breaks free and rolls for the sea. Crush everything to grow, but the bigger you get, the slower you roll, so split, throw, and slam your way back to speed. Eleven roads, one rule: don't stop.

Every road teaches one move and then mixes it with the ones before. JUMP the aqueduct gaps, or miss one and fall into a burning canyon far below; follow its winding road and ride the tailwind back up. SPLIT sheds weight and dashes through houses and ice walls. On the cliffs, HOLD while falling to smash the blue-roofed towers for a burst of speed. Grow on snow, THROW pieces of yourself at purple crystal walls (or jump them), ride the Tide Garden currents to go faster, and charge the golden pads at the Echo Altar to launch over wide gaps. Six hidden routes open only to players who arrive fast enough or use the right move.

Reach the sea for a time, score and medal. Beat 3:00 for gold or 4:00 for silver; every finish earns bronze. Retry the current road at any time, or start a fresh timed run.

# Controls
Drag to steer. Action buttons (JUMP, SPLIT, HOLD, THROW) appear on the right as you unlock them. HOLD brakes on the ground, slams in the air, and charges gold pads (let go on green). Best on a phone in portrait with two thumbs. Sound on!
