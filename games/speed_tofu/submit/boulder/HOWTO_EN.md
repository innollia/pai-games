Drag to steer; the stone rolls by itself. Crush pots to grow, but extra weight makes climbs, turns and jumps harder. Stay below the red speed mark too long and the run ends. At a fork, the glowing gold arrow shows the branch you will take.

Buttons unlock along the journey: JUMP, SPLIT, HOLD and THROW. SPLIT sheds weight and dashes through houses and ice walls. HOLD brakes on the ground; in the air it slams down. Slam the blue-roofed towers on the cliffs to break them and speed up (land on a roof without HOLD and you just scrape over it, slowly). THROW when the aiming line turns green to open purple crystal walls, or jump over them. Snow grows you; yellow lanes melt you.

Fall off the aqueduct and you drop into a burning canyon far below: stay on the winding stone road (the lava sizzles) and ride the golden tailwind back up. In the Tide Garden, ride the currents to gain speed, or HOLD to stay put. At the Echo Altar, HOLD on a gold pad until it turns green, then let go to launch.

Reach the sea for your time, score and medal. Gold: 3:00 or faster. Silver: 4:00 or faster. Bronze: finish. Swipe right, left, left, up on the screen to open the shortcut window.
