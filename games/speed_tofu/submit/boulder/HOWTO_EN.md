Drag to steer; the temple stone rolls automatically. Crush pots to grow. Extra weight makes climbs, turns and jumps harder. Staying below the red speed mark for 1.5 seconds ends the run.

Buttons unlock along the journey: jump, split, hold to brake or dive, and throw. Throw when aiming dots turn green to open purple crystal walls. Snow grows you; yellow sun lanes melt you. Aqueduct falls lead to a lower route and its yellow exit. Split before fragile bridges.

In the Tide Garden, arrows show sideways currents. Hold to resist them or jump out; smaller stones drift more. Its mixing section combines currents with snow, throwing and gap jumps.

At the Resonance Altar, hold on a gold pressure bed until its charge reaches 40% and turns green, then release to launch across the wide gap. Keep released while airborne; holding again dives. Its mixing section adds snow, crushing and a crystal wall after landing.

Combine everything at the sea shrine. Reaching the sea triggers a final camera move and time/score result. Gold: 3:00 or faster. Silver: 4:00 or faster. Bronze: finish. Best completion time and highest score persist locally. Zone retries retain elapsed play time and failure count, including first-zone retries; choose “처음부터” for a fresh timed run.
