Push the stick right to run, tap JUMP to hop. Win a round by DELIVERING the most parcels: when a red mailbox is on screen the BOX button turns green — tap it and your parcel flies in (each mailbox takes one parcel). The gold mailbox before the finish is worth 3, but whoever reaches it first can't use it. Tap BOX anywhere else to throw a parcel backward for a speed dash (0.9 s cooldown); thrown parcels land on the road for rivals to grab.

The camera follows the leader. Run fast in front and you see less ahead — easy to overshoot mailboxes. Following close behind shows you the mailboxes coming.

You start each round with 8 parcels stacked on your back; each one slows you a little. Fall off the torn LEFT edge and you drop a parcel and climb back in (with an empty back you get one parcel back). The side walls of the screen are made of every parcel being carried; as parcels are delivered, thrown or lost, the screen gets narrower. Falling behind gives you a catch-up boost; if you stop playing, a "Give up?" button appears.

When the first courier crosses the finish, 3 seconds of LAST CALL, then the round ends. Six towns, 2 round wins each, then the final delivery. Modes: Classic, Night Lantern (mailboxes show only in the leader's light), Rush (4 or fewer parcels: ceiling boost and wall bounce). Earn gold/silver/bronze per town and beat your best delivery count.
