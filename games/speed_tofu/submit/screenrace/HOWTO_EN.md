Run right with the joystick, tap JUMP to jump. The camera follows whoever is in front — if you fall off the LEFT edge of the screen you lose a parcel; with no parcels left you're out.

Parcels (boxes on your back) make you slower and jump lower, but they keep you alive at the edge. Tap PARCEL to throw one away for a quick burst. You need at least one parcel to cross the finish. The screen is as wide as all parcels being carried.

The leader can't see far ahead when running fast. Each course has its own rule, shown at the start. Win 2 rounds to clear a course; between rounds pick the next route.
