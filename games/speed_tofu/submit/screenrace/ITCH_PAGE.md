# Title
Off the Screen! / 화면 밖 경주

# Short description
A four-courier race where the camera follows the leader — fall off the left edge and you're out.

# Long description
Off the Screen! — race three rival couriers across six bright courses. The camera chases whoever is in front, and anyone pushed off the left edge of the screen loses a parcel. Lose them all and you're out.

Parcels are your shield and your weight: carry more and you run slower and jump lower, but the edge only takes a parcel instead of you. Tap PARCEL to throw one away for a burst of speed. The screen is only as wide as all the parcels being carried, so the race gets tighter as everyone sheds weight. Lead too fast and your own view ahead shrinks.

Each course plays differently: flowing riverbanks, a railway crossing with a train coming at you, blackouts in the night market, a rope bridge that cracks under heavy runners, rooftops with no walls. Between rounds pick the next route — low gate, ice slide or headwind — and your parcels and rank carry over.

Modes: Classic, and Night Lantern (the leader holds the only light; run too fast and it shakes).

# Controls
Joystick left/right to run, JUMP button, PARCEL button (drop one for a burst). Portrait, touch only. Sound on!
