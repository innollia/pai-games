STYLE: Pocket Parcel Town (Off the Screen! game art)
ONE LINE: Cut-paper diorama illustration of chunky folded-paper toy couriers, matte gouache washes on layered colored paper and cardboard.
FORMAT: single game title illustration, landscape 4:3, full-bleed
SUBJECT POLICY: KEEP
RENDER:
- Every shape is a cut or folded piece of colored paper or cardboard with a thin white paper edge.
- Shapes overlap in a few flat layers like a shallow paper diorama; soft small contact shadows under each layer.
- Matte gouache and watercolor washes, a few broad confident brush marks per shape.
- Couriers are paper toys with big heads: large rectangular folded-paper cap with a short brim, peach face, one dark dot eye in profile, small forward nose, short jacket, navy trousers, mitten hands, chunky brown running shoes.
- Cap and jacket colors name the courier: coral #E86758 (the player), blue #508CC8, mint #62AD91, gold #E8B34B. Skin and shoes stay the same on all couriers.
- Parcels are taped cardboard boxes #DDA56A with a red tape cross, stacked in one tall column on the courier's back, behind the head.
- Mailboxes are red paper boxes on a navy post with a small cream address label.
- Side view, couriers run to the right.
- Warm top-left daylight, painted as warm upper bevels and cool lower folds.
DENSITY: Detail on the couriers, their parcel stacks and the mailbox; the street behind is a few simplified paper shapes; sky is flat warm paper.
PALETTE: warm paper cream #FFF0D5, cardboard #DDA56A, tape #F8D58A, dark ink #34334B, dusty coral, muted apricot, sage; foreground more saturated than background.
PRESERVE:
- Paper-toy couriers with countable parcel stacks on their backs.
- Thin white paper edges on cut shapes.
- Clear foreground/background separation, bright cheerful daylight.
STYLE NEGATIVES:
- No realistic human proportions; couriers stay big-headed paper toys.
- No glossy plastic toy look or clay look.
- No photographic paper texture or random fine paper noise.
- No dark or night lighting.
- No parcels baked into the courier's body; parcels sit as separate boxes on the back.
